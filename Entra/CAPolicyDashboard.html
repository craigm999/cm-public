    Policies

    Overlap is worked out from what each policy targets directly. Group and role membership isn't expanded, so two policies targeting different groups that share members aren't shown as overlapping. "Office 365" and "Microsoft Admin Portals" are treated as single targets, as are named locations. Device and application filters aren't evaluated.

    Overlapping policies aren't necessarily wrong: Entra applies every policy that matches and the user must satisfy all of them. Overlaps with the same controls, or identical scope, are the ones most likely to be redundant.
