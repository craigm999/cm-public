          Applications

          “Never signed in” means the sign-in activity report has no record for the app. That report only covers a limited history, and apps created recently may not have been used yet; check the Created column.

          App registrations take the sign-in activity of their enterprise app (service principal) in this tenant. App registrations with no enterprise app here show as never signed in.

          Recent users (when the report is run with -IncludeRecentUsers) come from the sign-in logs (last 30 days at most): the most recent distinct users who signed in successfully, interactively or not, to the app or to it as an API. Apps with no user sign-ins in that period have none listed.
