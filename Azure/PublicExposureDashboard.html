      Coverage of the report.

      Severity: Critical – reachable now from any internet address on a management, database, file-sharing or container port (or every port), or data readable without signing in. High – a likely way in (no WAF, public Kubernetes API, database open to broad ranges or all networks, RDP/SSH published through a load balancer). Medium – public but should be restricted, or a weak setting (HTTP, WAF in detection mode, all networks allowed). Low – restricted exposure or hygiene. Info – public by design and configured as expected.

      VM reachability is worked out from the NIC and subnet NSGs (rules in priority order, including the defaults; a Basic public IP with no NSG is open, a Standard one closed). It doesn't evaluate application security groups as sources, service tags other than Internet, or an Azure Firewall / NVA in front, so treat VM findings as "the NSGs would allow this".
